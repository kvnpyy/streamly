=== PIN in #help ===

Before you ask — quick checks:

1. Do you have an active subscription with your IPTV provider? Streamly can't fix expired panels.
2. Xtream login: server URL (with http/https), username, password — same as your provider gave you.
3. M3U: paste the full playlist URL your provider sent (often ends in .m3u or get.php?...).
4. Streams won't play? Try another browser (Chrome vs Safari). Live HLS varies by provider.
5. TV browser: use iptvwebplayer.org in the TV's browser, sign in, then pair if needed in Settings.

Don't post your full password in public. Redact it or DM a mod if you must share details.

Site: https://iptvwebplayer.org/login
Settings (after login): https://iptvwebplayer.org/app/settings
