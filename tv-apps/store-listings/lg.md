## Short description

## Full description

Streamly turns your LG webOS TV into an IPTV front-end for your existing subscription. Log in with Xtream Codes or an M3U URL from your provider — Streamly does not sell channels.

• Live TV with EPG and channel browsing
• Movies and series with continue watching
• PIN pairing from your phone — no remote typing
• Built for the Magic Remote and large-screen UI

Requires your own IPTV subscription. Visit https://iptvwebplayer.org/tv for setup instructions.
