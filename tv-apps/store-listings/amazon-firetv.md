## Long description

Streamly is a web-based IPTV player designed for Fire TV and your remote. Connect your own Xtream or M3U subscription — we do not provide channels.

Features:
• Live TV with program guide
• Movies and series libraries with resume
• PIN pairing — sign in from your phone
• Optimized for Fire TV Silk browser

You must have an active subscription from an IPTV provider. Streamly is only the player.

TV setup: https://iptvwebplayer.org/tv
